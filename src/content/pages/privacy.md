_Last updated: 11 June 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains how rapjumping.com ("we", "us") handles personal information, in line with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

**The short version:** rapjumping.com is a simple content website. We have no user accounts, no contact or signup forms, and no advertising or cross-site tracking. We do not set tracking cookies.

**What we collect**

We do not ask you for personal information, and there are no accounts or forms on this site. We collect only limited technical data automatically:

- **Cloudflare Web Analytics** - a privacy-first, cookieless analytics tool that records aggregated, non-identifying data such as page views, approximate country, referrer, and browser/device type. It does not use cookies and does not track you across other websites.
- **Standard server logs** - like all websites, our infrastructure provider (Cloudflare) automatically logs technical request data including IP address and user-agent string, which it retains for a limited period for security, abuse prevention and performance.

**Why we collect it**

We use this limited data only to:

- keep the site secure and protect against abuse;
- monitor and improve site performance and reliability; and
- understand, in aggregate, which articles readers find useful.

**Cookies**

This site does not set any tracking or advertising cookies. Our analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics) is cookieless. This statement also serves as our cookie disclosure - there is no separate cookie policy.

**Overseas disclosure**

Our site is served through Cloudflare's global content-delivery and security network. This means the limited technical data above may be processed on servers located outside Australia, including in the United States.

**Access, correction and complaints**

Because we do not hold accounts or identifiable profiles, there is usually no personal information for us to retrieve or correct. If you have a privacy question, or wish to make a request or complaint, contact us at [privacy@rapjumping.com](mailto:privacy@rapjumping.com) and we will respond within a reasonable time.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may escalate your complaint to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at [oaic.gov.au](https://www.oaic.gov.au).

**Changes to this policy**

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above reflects the most recent version.
