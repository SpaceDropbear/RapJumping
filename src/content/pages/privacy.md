_Last updated: 23 September 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains how rapjumping.com ("we", "us") handles personal information, in line with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

**The short version:** rapjumping.com is a content and affiliate website. We have no user accounts and no contact or signup forms. We use privacy-conscious analytics to understand how the site is used, and we link to third-party tour operators and gear retailers through affiliate programs; some of those links earn us a small commission. We do not sell your personal information.

**What we collect**

We do not ask you for personal information, and there are no accounts or forms on this site. We collect only limited technical data automatically:

- **Cloudflare Web Analytics** - a privacy-first analytics tool run by our hosting provider, Cloudflare, that records aggregated, non-identifying data such as page views, approximate country, referrer, and browser/device type.
- **Google Analytics 4 (GA4)** - we use GA4 to understand aggregate visitor behaviour (which pages are read, how long for, and which outbound links are clicked, including affiliate links) and to measure whether our affiliate partnerships are working. GA4 may use first-party cookies or similar device identifiers to distinguish visits; it does not collect your name, email or any other information that identifies you personally.
- **Standard server logs** - like all websites, our infrastructure provider (Cloudflare) automatically logs technical request data including IP address and user-agent string, which it retains for a limited period for security, abuse prevention and performance.

**Why we collect it**

We use this limited data only to:

- keep the site secure and protect against abuse;
- monitor and improve site performance and reliability;
- understand, in aggregate, which articles readers find useful; and
- measure how our affiliate links perform, so we know which recommendations are actually useful to readers.

**Affiliate links and how they earn**

Some links on this site - to gear retailers and to third-party tour and activity operators - are affiliate links, run through affiliate networks and programs including **GetYourGuide**, **Commission Factory**, and **impact.com**. If you click one of these links and go on to book or buy something, we may earn a small commission from the retailer or operator. This costs you nothing extra, and it does not affect the price you pay. We only link to products, tours or operators we think are genuinely useful to readers, and the recommendation is chosen before any affiliate link is attached to it.

Affiliate links are marked in the page as sponsored (`rel="sponsored"`) and sit inside a labelled box ("Our picks" or "Book it"), so you can always see when a link is commercial. Clicking through takes you to the retailer's or operator's own website, and **they, not us, may set their own cookies or tracking identifiers on their site** to record that the visit came from rapjumping.com, so the commission can be attributed correctly. We do not control, and are not responsible for, those third parties' own privacy practices - check their policies if you have concerns.

**Cookies**

rapjumping.com does not set any advertising or cross-site-tracking cookies of its own. Our Cloudflare Web Analytics is cookieless. Google Analytics 4 may set first-party cookies on this domain for the purpose described above. If you click through an affiliate link, the retailer or tour operator's own site may set its own cookies once you land there - that happens on their domain, not ours. This statement also serves as our cookie disclosure - there is no separate cookie policy.

**Overseas disclosure**

Our site is served through Cloudflare's global content-delivery and security network, and our analytics is processed by Google. This means the limited technical data above may be processed on servers located outside Australia, including in the United States.

**Access, correction and complaints**

Because we do not hold accounts or identifiable profiles, there is usually no personal information for us to retrieve or correct. If you have a privacy question, or wish to make a request or complaint, contact us at [privacy@rapjumping.com](mailto:privacy@rapjumping.com) or [info@rapjumping.com](mailto:info@rapjumping.com) and we will respond within a reasonable time.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may escalate your complaint to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at [oaic.gov.au](https://www.oaic.gov.au).

**Changes to this policy**

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above reflects the most recent version.
