If you've been searching for an abseiling podcast to guide your next adventure, you've come to the right place. Whether you're a seasoned climber or an enthusiastic newcomer, hearing other people's experiences with rope descents, gear tips, and behind-the-scenes stories can boost your confidence on the wall. Podcasts are easy to listen to on commutes, long drives, or lazy weekends, so they're an ideal way to deepen your understanding of abseiling (also called rappelling) without the pressure of formal training. On top of that, books can give you step-by-step methods and carefully researched insights. Put both together, and you'll have a powerhouse toolkit for learning, improving, and, most importantly, staying safe.

Below, you'll discover a curated list of top podcasts covering abseiling, climbing, and the broader adventure scene. You'll also find recommended reads to enhance your technical know-how. Ready to tune in and dive into some practical knowledge? Let's go.

## Learn why podcasts matter

Podcasts have soared in popularity because they put expert-level discussions right in your ear. They're conversational, meaning you get the relaxed vibe of friends chatting about their best abseiling mishaps, gear discoveries, and successful climbs. Podcasts also offer variety:

- You can listen while tackling your morning workout.
- You'll hear real-life experiences, not just textbook theory.
- You're free to pause, replay, and take notes on the go.

In other words, a thoughtful abseiling podcast can become your personal coaching session. Picture a friend talking through how to control friction on the rope or manage your fear when stepping over the edge. You'll swap theories and stories without feeling rushed, and that makes learning genuinely enjoyable.

## Discover top abseiling podcasts

### The RunOut Podcast

Hosted by Chris Kalous (known from The Enormocast) and Andrew Bisharat (Evening Sends), The RunOut Podcast offers a laid-back but deeply informative look at the climbing world. Even if the show's main focus is broader climbing culture, you'll still come across valuable discussions on core techniques like abseiling and rappelling.

- Key topic: Climbing's cultural heartbeat, from bouldering to ice climbing.
- Why it's great: Insightful interviews with seasoned climbers who aren't shy about the nitty-gritty details of gear, training, and safety.
- Notable guests: Pro climbers such as Emily Harrington, Mark Synnott, and Sonnie Trotter.

Expect witty banter, no-nonsense advice, and the occasional diverging opinion on what's “right” or “traditional” in climbing. It's an entertaining way to sharpen your rope skills and learn from experts who've pretty much seen it all.

### The Adventure Podcast (Episode 196 with Leo Houlding)

If you're looking for big wall stories, expedition insights, and a healthy dose of lived experience, tune in to Episode 196 of The Adventure Podcast. It features Leo Houlding, a top-level climber, alpinist, and father who's explored everything from Antarctica to Baffin Island.

- Key topic: Global expeditions and personal evolution as a climber.
- Why it's great: Leo opens up about everything from growing up in a barn to becoming a father, all while pulling off some of the most challenging climbs in the world.
- Abseiling angle: Managing descents in far-flung locations, dealing with rope setups, and balancing risk on remote mountains.

This episode was originally recorded live online, which adds a fun, interactive vibe as you listen. Whether you dream of your first big cliff or you're planning an advanced route, hearing Leo's perspective might nudge you to push your boundaries, one (carefully secured) step at a time.

### The Enormocast

Chris Kalous also hosts The Enormocast, a climbing-focused show frequently praised for its relatable humour and honest conversations. While it may not dedicate entire episodes solely to abseiling, episodes often feature rope management tips and personal stories that revolve around descending safely.

- Key topic: Interview-driven look at the climbing lifestyle.
- Why it's great: You hear from professional climbers, route setters, and weekend warriors who share raw anecdotes-mistakes included.
- Standout moments: In-depth discussions on technique, gear, and the joys (and miseries) of vertical life.

If you're a future guide, club member, or frequent hobbyist, The Enormocast can help solidify the fundamental idea that abseiling technique is crucial, whether you're retreating off a climb or practising at the crag.

## Check out these books

While podcasts keep you company on the move, books let you dig deeper into the how-tos and whys. Think step-by-step diagrams for rope handling, detailed safety checklists, and historical perspectives on abseiling. Below are a few widely respected resources in the climbing community. They're not exclusively about abseiling, but they contain relevant chapters or sections that will level up your rope-work skills.

### Mountaineering: The Freedom of the Hills

Often called the bible of mountaineering, this classic text covers nearly every skill you need for high-altitude adventures, including abseiling. Originally published decades ago and now in multiple revised editions, it remains a staple on many climbers' bookshelves.

- Topics covered: Knot tying, rope team travel, anchor systems, rappelling, and rescue.
- Why it's helpful: It demystifies advanced topics, so even if you're just starting, you'll find entry-level advice explained in plain language.

Because it's not exclusively about abseiling, you'll also learn how rope skills fit seamlessly into broader mountaineering. That knowledge can be invaluable if you plan to expand beyond single-pitch climbs.

### Rock Climbing: Mastering Basic Skills (Craig Luebben)

Craig Luebben's approachable style and thorough visual aids make this a must-read for new climbers who want to ensure they start with a solid foundation. Although the focus is on rock climbing, you'll discover sections dedicated to belaying, rappelling, and essential rope maneuvers.

- Topics covered: Fundamental climbing techniques, belay safety, abseiling setups.
- Why it's helpful: Clear illustrations break down complicated techniques, so you can practise efficiently, even without an instructor by your side.

Luebben aims to reduce intimidation and confusion by keeping instructions simple and direct. If you're into self-study, this is an excellent place to build confidence in your rope handling.

### Self-Rescue (David J. Fasulo)

Ever wonder what happens if things go wrong mid-pitch? Fasulo's book zeroes in on problem-solving strategies that climbers and abseilers can use to manage unexpected challenges. If you're curious about how you'd handle a stuck rope or a jammed device on a cliff face, this resource offers step-by-step solutions.

- Topics covered: Rescue techniques, escaping the belay, improvised rope systems, advanced rappelling.
- Why it's helpful: It trains you to think calmly in high-pressure situations, a skill that can literally save lives.

Though it's more advanced in scope, you can still benefit from reading it early in your climbing journey. It reminds you that thorough training and a sense of caution are your best friends when you're hanging off the side of a cliff.

## Join an abseiling community

Reading and listening to experts can get you far, but nothing beats real-life practice in a supportive group. If you're based in Australia-or planning a visit-check out the abseiling community australia. Local communities often hold workshops, group outings, and social events that fast-track trust-building and skill-development. You'll swap stories and gear tips in person, make new climbing friends, and gain confidence under the watchful eyes of experienced members.

Joining or forming a club also helps you:

- Confirm you're using your gear properly
- Double-check knots and anchors for increased safety
- Find climbing partners and share guide recommendations
- Access resources like training scholarships or discounted passes

Community-based learning often gives you real feedback about your technique. Plus, there's that irreplaceable feeling of camaraderie when you celebrate each other's first successful abseil or a new personal best.

## Wrap up your knowledge

There's a reason so many climbers call abseiling “controlled falling.” You want adequate friction on the rope, proper anchor setups, and an extra margin of safety, especially when you're hundreds of feet off the ground. Learning from a solid abseiling podcast, backed by trusted climbing books, teaches you how to manage those risks and helps you avoid the common pitfalls of hasty descents.

Don't forget to set clear personal goals before you press play or crack open the first page. Maybe you want to transition from gym climbing to multi-pitch routes, or you're aiming to become a certified guide one day. Either way, a blend of real-world mentorship, online audio learning, and honest reading-even if it's just a chapter a week-gives you a serious advantage.

Below is a quick recap on how to make the most of these curated resources:

- Start with a podcast episode that grabs your interest. Let the stories inspire you, and note any gear tips you can research later.
- Choose at least one book from the list to build your technical foundation. Abseiling is more straightforward when you understand the science of friction and proper anchor placement.
- Seek out in-person meets or training sessions. Post on forums or local groups to find others who share your interests.
- Continuously review your technique. Small adjustments can drastically reduce fatigue or friction burns.
- Never ignore safety. A thoroughly inspected rope, properly fitted harness, and a reliable anchor are non-negotiable.

Sharing your experiences-both good and embarrassing-enriches the entire climbing community. So be open: talk about your first shaky abseil or the time you forgot your gloves. You'll inspire others to learn from your mistakes and remind yourself just how far you've come.

Happy descending, and remember-you're never alone on the rope when you have the wisdom of expert climbers, podcast hosts, and published authors right by your side!
