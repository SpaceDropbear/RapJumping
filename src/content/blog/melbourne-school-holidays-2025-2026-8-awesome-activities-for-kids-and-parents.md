The Victorian summer school holidays officially begin on Friday, December 19th, 2025, just a few days before Christmas. While the kids are sure to be preoccupied with the imminent arrival of everyone's favourite jolly man, post-Christmas is another story!

Once the presents are played with and the tinsel is packed away, how are you going to keep the kids entertained for the remaining month of their school break (until Term 1, 2026 begins on January 28th)? Well, the good news is that Melbourne has a fantastic array of options on offer for bored children - and parents in need of a well-deserved reprieve!

Here are some of our favourite suggestions for awesome **Melbourne activities** to fill those long summer days:

---

## **1. Go Stargazing at Melbourne Observatory**

View the spectacular Australian night sky from the historic Melbourne Observatory, nestled within the Royal Botanic Gardens. Using powerful heritage telescopes, experts from the Astronomical Society of Victoria guide visitors on an awe-inspiring journey through the southern sky. It's an enchanting and educational experience for all ages, perfect for sparking curiosity about the universe.

*Tours should be booked in advance via the Astronomical Society of Victoria website and typically go ahead regardless of weather conditions.*

## **2. Hit the Ice at O'Brien Icehouse**

Want to go ice skating in the middle of summer? Thanks to O'Brien Icehouse (formerly O'Brien Group Arena) in Docklands, you can! Throughout the school holidays, they offer various programs suitable for children of all ages and skill levels. For children 12 years of age and up, activities like 'Bumperballs on Ice' (strapping into an inflatable bubble suit for a unique game of soccer) are often a sure-fire hit, adding a hilarious twist to classic ice skating.

*Located conveniently in Docklands, the arena offers public skating every day from 9 am until late, and entry often includes a variety of free lessons held throughout the day.*

## **3. Return to the Riverbank with Mr. Toad & Friends!**

Kenneth Grahame's beloved classic, *The Wind in the Willows*, often returns to the enchanting setting of the Royal Botanic Gardens each summer. This critically acclaimed, interactive outdoor theatre production, often presented by the Australian Shakespeare Company, is brimming with music, laughter, and charming characters like Mr. Toad, Ratty, Mole, and Badger. It's a magical way to spend a summer afternoon immersed in a classic story amidst beautiful natural surroundings, popular with children and adults alike.

*Bookings for these highly anticipated performances can typically be made in advance via the production company's direct site or the Royal Botanic Gardens website once dates are announced.*

## **4. Dive into Digital Worlds at ACMI**

Located at Federation Square, the **Australian Centre for the Moving Image (ACMI)** offers an exciting escape into the world of film, TV, video games, and digital art. It's a fantastic indoor option, perfect for hot summer days, and often hosts special school holiday programs and interactive exhibitions that engage kids of all ages. From exploring the history of gaming to creating their own stop-motion animations or immersing themselves in virtual realities, ACMI provides a unique blend of entertainment and education that sparks creativity and critical thinking.

*Entry to the main 'Screen Worlds' exhibition is often free, with special exhibitions or holiday programs sometimes requiring a ticket. Check the ACMI website for their latest programs and booking information.*

## **5.** Unleash Their Inner Climber: The Perfect School Holiday Adventure!

Looking for an exciting and active way to beat school holiday boredom? **Clip 'n Climb Berwick** offers an incredible indoor climbing **adventure** that's perfect for kids of all ages and skill levels! Watch your child's confidence soar as they tackle over 20 unique and vibrant climbing challenges in a safe, supervised environment. It's the ideal way to burn off energy, develop problem-solving skills, and create unforgettable memories, no matter the weather. Don't let the holidays pass by without a dose of thrilling fun and physical activity.

*Visit the Clip 'n Climb Berwick website today to check session times, age requirements, and book your family's next great adventure!*

## **6. Meet Melbourne's Friendliest Sea Life at SEA LIFE Melbourne Aquarium**

The **SEA LIFE Melbourne Aquarium**, located on King Street, offers endless entertainment these school holidays, especially on hot summer days. From the aquarium's colourful underwater residents to interactive exhibits and themed zones, there really is something for everyone. Get up close and personal with playful penguins, marvel at massive sharks, or discover the magical world of seahorses and rays!

*Pre-book your tickets online via the SEA LIFE Melbourne Aquarium website for potential discounts and to guarantee entry during peak holiday periods.*

## **7. Climb High into the Treetops with Trees Adventure**

Kids love to climb, and with **Trees Adventure**, they can do it safely and spectacularly! With parks located across Victoria (including one conveniently close to the Melbourne CBD in Belgrave), children (and adults!) can navigate a challenging obstacle course that winds its way through the canopies.

Complete with secure safety harnesses and experienced instructors, this activity allows thrill-seekers to climb tall trees, fly through the air via zip lines, and even tackle airborne challenges like wobbly bridges and suspended skateboards. It's a fantastic way to combine physical activity with the thrill of being high up!

*Sessions typically span two hours and include training. Bookings are essential, so check their website for park locations and availability.*
