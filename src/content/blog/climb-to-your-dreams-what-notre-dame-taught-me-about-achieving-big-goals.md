Visitors to **Paris, France**, often find themselves drawn to the majestic **Notre Dame Cathedral**. Beyond its stunning façade and intricate history, one of the hidden adventures awaits those brave enough to climb to the top of its iconic towers for a panoramic view of the city.

The ascent is no small feat. The staircase is notoriously narrow and steep - a true testament to medieval architecture that predates modern building codes and the average height of today's individuals. It's not unusual for visitors to pause, catch their breath, and question their life choices amidst the endless brick and stone. Yet, inevitably, they push on. And once at the summit, the breathtaking view of Paris makes every arduous step worth it.

It's tempting to quit halfway, especially if you're battling fatigue or have mobility challenges. But often, the gentle encouragement (or competitive nudge) from fellow climbers behind you keeps the queue moving. The most amazing part? As you near the top, a beam of light from an opening in the upper wall signals the end is in sight. Suddenly, renewed energy surges, and you push even faster, knowing the reward is just steps away.

This experience offers a powerful parallel to the journey of manifesting a significant goal.

---

## The Hidden Truth About Achieving "Big, Hairy, Audacious Goals"

The path to achieving any truly significant goal often feels like that climb up Notre Dame's tower. It's composed of numerous small, sometimes mundane, and occasionally boring steps. You might feel like one step is just like the last, with no real progress in sight. At these moments, quitting can seem like the most intelligent, logical choice.

However, just like that glimpse of light at the top of the tower, once a piece of your goal comes into view - a small victory, a breakthrough, or just a clearer vision - it powerfully reinforces your choice to take just one more step.

So, how do you maintain that crucial motivation when the path feels long and tedious, especially when tackling what authors James Collins and Jerry Porras refer to as a "Big, Hairy Audacious Goal" (BHAG)? There are two key principles that can help you maintain momentum and accelerate your manifestation.

---

## Principle 1: Visualize Your Goal Daily (and Feel It!)

This isn't just about seeing your goal; it's about experiencing it with every sense. The more tangible you can make a distant goal feel, the more reachable it becomes, fuelling your motivation.

- **Go Beyond Sight:** If your goal is a better job, don't just imagine a new office. Imagine and feel what it would be like to be thriving in that perfect role, in the ideal location.
- **Engage Every Sense:**
  - **Sight:** See yourself receiving positive feedback from clients, or walking into your dream office space.
  - **Sound:** Hear the celebratory cheers, the hum of productivity, or the appreciation from colleagues.
  - **Touch:** Feel the firmness of a handshake as you sign a new deal, or the comfort of a new desk chair.
  - **Smell:** Think about that "new car smell" at a dealership - it's designed to make a product feel real and desirable. What does success smell like? Fresh coffee in your new office? The scent of a beautiful park near your dream home?
  - **Taste:** What does success taste like? A celebratory meal?
- **Embrace the Emotion:** Crucially, feel the emotions associated with achieving your goal: the joy, the relief, the pride, the financial security. This emotional connection is a powerful motivator.

By incorporating sound, taste, touch, and smell into your daily visualization sessions, you transform a distant aspiration into a vibrant, compelling reality in your mind. This makes taking those tedious steps feel less like a chore and more like a direct path to an almost-tangible future.

---

## Principle 2: Find Your Accountability Buddy

It's surprisingly easy to use self-talk to rationalise avoiding the next, possibly scary, step toward a goal. We can make countless excuses to ourselves. It's not so easy when there's another person involved.

- **The Power of Gentle Prodding:** An accountability buddy provides that reciprocal, friendly coaxing (or yes, sometimes gentle nagging) that keeps you both moving forward. Knowing someone else is relying on you, or checking in on your progress, adds an extra layer of commitment.
- **Shared Journey, Shared Fun:** This shared journey can significantly accelerate manifestation for both individuals. It also injects a vital element of fun into what might otherwise be a solitary, demanding process. You can celebrate small wins together, commiserate over setbacks, and offer fresh perspectives when one of you feels stuck.
- **Finding Your Match:** Look for someone who shares similar ambitions, or simply someone you trust and respect, who is also committed to their own goals. Regular check-ins - daily texts, weekly calls, or even monthly coffee meetings in a vibrant Melbourne café - can make all the difference.

---

## Your Climb to Success Starts Now

Manifesting a "Big, Hairy Audacious Goal" may indeed take weeks, months, or even years. Maintaining the motivation to continue that journey is absolutely crucial for success.

Remember the Notre Dame climb: it's okay to pause along the way, to catch your breath. But then, take just one more step. Keep your ultimate view in sight, leverage the power of vivid visualization, and lean on an accountability partner to keep you moving.

Just like the breathtaking panorama from the top of that ancient tower, the view from the summit of your own achievements will be well worth every single step. So, what big goal are you ready to climb towards in 2025?
