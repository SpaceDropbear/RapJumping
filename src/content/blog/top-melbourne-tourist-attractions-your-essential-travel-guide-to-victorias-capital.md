Melbourne, Australia's second-largest city, is affectionately known as "Marvellous Melbourne" by those who live here. Often dubbed "Australia's Garden City" due to its abundance of parks and lush gardens, Melbourne is a vivacious hub brimming with **culture** and **history**. It's no wonder that **tourists** from around the world flock to this modern metropolis to relax, rejuvenate, and explore its unique charm.

Located gracefully near Port Phillip Bay and nestled along the banks of the iconic Yarra River, Melbourne boasts grand views and captivating water-centred attractions. It offers elegance and wonder to every visitor, at every stage of life. Tour the numerous **historical sites in Melbourne**, or take a serene stroll through the lush greenery of the Royal Botanic Gardens. It would genuinely take several **visits to Melbourne** to fully discover all that this incredible city has to offer.

So, for a first-time **tourist in Melbourne**, where is the best place to go? What are the absolute **must-see attractions**? While it's impossible to list everything, here are eight choice options, each as marvellous as the next, to kickstart your **Melbourne adventure**.

---

## Federation Square

No **visit to Melbourne** is complete without stopping at Federation Square; in fact, it's where most people begin their exploration. Opened in 2002 to commemorate 100 years of federation, its striking, deconstructed design contrasts elegantly with Melbourne's Victorian architecture, creating a vibrant public space. Federation Square hosts over 2,000 events annually and is renowned as Australia's largest Wi-Fi hotspot, drawing thousands of **visitors to Melbourne** each year. It's the pulsating heart of the city's public life.

---

### Melbourne Cricket Ground (MCG)

The Melbourne Cricket Ground (MCG) is frequently touted as one of the grandest stadiums in the world. **Visitors to Melbourne** can often catch thrilling cricket matches during the summer months and Australian Rules Football in the winter. Daily 75-minute tours are offered for guests, providing an insight into its rich sporting **history**. The MCG famously hosted the 1956 Olympic Games and frequently doubles as a premier venue for major concerts and events throughout the year, making it a key **Melbourne tourist attraction**.

---

### Eureka Skydeck

The Eureka Tower is a highly popular attraction that pierces the sky, standing a colossal 91 stories above ground. The Eureka Skydeck, located on the 88th floor, offers the highest public lookout in the Southern Hemisphere. Named after the Eureka Stockade, its gold-plated windows still sparkle brilliantly in the sun, symbolizing Melbourne's gold rush past. There's a unique thrill in ascending to the 88th floor and taking in the breathtaking panoramic **views of Melbourne** - an unforgettable **experience**.

---

### Melbourne Museum and Royal Exhibition Building

This modern Melbourne Museum is home to a diverse and fascinating collection. Explore everything from natural environments to Australian cultural **history**, offering engaging exhibits for all ages. Included in the gallery is a series of thought-provoking, hands-on activities designed to captivate children, even offering "romp and stomp" fun days.

The majestic Royal Exhibition Building, a UNESCO World Heritage site, sits directly across from the Melbourne Museum. Built in 1880, this iconic structure held the first Commonwealth Parliament of Australia, making it a significant landmark for Australian **history** and an essential stop for **tourists**.

---

### Melbourne Zoo

No **trip to Melbourne** would be complete without a visit to the Melbourne Zoo. It is home to over 300 different species of animals, offering incredible viewing pleasure for the public. The zoo provides many unique **adventure** experiences, including "roar and snore" overnight stays and twilight music concerts. It even offers exclusive behind-the-scenes tours of certain enclosures. People of all ages consistently enjoy visiting the Melbourne Zoo, making it a fantastic **family-friendly Melbourne attraction**.

---

### Yarra River Cruise

A Yarra River cruise is the perfect way to take in the city's sights and absorb some local **history**. Glide along the scenic banks of the Yarra River or cruise over to historic Williamstown. You can opt for a leisurely dinner cruise, or simply catch a ferry for a unique commute. Several popular cruise companies operate along the Yarra River, offering scenic tours filled with rich culture and fascinating historical anecdotes. Many even provide private and **corporate cruises** overlooking the picturesque river, making it a versatile **Melbourne experience**.

---

### Docklands

Docklands is a vibrant waterfront satellite village, just minutes from the city's bustling centre, packed with trendy cafes and restaurants. There's always something dynamic happening here. It boasts the highest concentration of Green Star-rated buildings in the Southern Hemisphere, reflecting its modern sustainable design. Docklands also features a world-class ice sports venue and a giant observation wheel, offering stunning city views. Many Sundays, it hosts a lively art and vintage market, adding to its appeal as a contemporary **Melbourne attraction**.

---

### National Gallery of Victoria (NGV)

The National Gallery of Victoria (NGV) is Australia's oldest and most visited art gallery, an absolute must for art and **culture** enthusiasts. Comprising two magnificent buildings - NGV International on St Kilda Road and The Ian Potter Centre: NGV Australia at Federation Square - it houses an extensive collection of Australian and international art. From ancient artifacts to contemporary masterpieces, the NGV offers a diverse range of exhibitions, often free, making it an accessible and enriching **Melbourne tourist attraction** for all ages.
